How it works

Three calls, one cutting layout.

Submit measurements and a style, get back nested pieces, a DXF, and a plain-language cutting sheet, all as one async job you poll until it's done.

01

Draft

Bust, waist, hip and length measurements go in. Stitchfren drafts the sloper pieces for your chosen style and adds seam allowance automatically.

02

Nest

Every piece is placed at the true bottom-left-most position of its no-fit-polygon against everything already on the roll, largest piece first, both grain-safe orientations tried.

03

Export

Get back a DXF at real scale, an SVG preview of the layout, and a cutting sheet with the fabric saved versus a naive row-by-row bake.

Main skill

Draft & nest a pattern

The core call: measurements and a style in, a fully nested, fabric-savings-verified layout out. Everything below supports this one job.

Drafting

Sloper pattern drafting

8 styles drafted straight from measurements: bodice tops, A-line and straight dresses and skirts, sleeved variants, and men's shirts in long or short sleeve, all with automatic seam allowance.

Nesting

True NFP fabric nesting

Minkowski-sum no-fit-polygon placement, not bounding-box packing. Deterministic, zero-overlap, grain-safe rotation.

Export

DXF export

Real-scale, cut-ready DXF for a plotter, laser cutter, or CAD import.

Instructions

Cutting sheet generation

A plain-language cutting sheet with every job, rule-based by default, with an optional LLM-written narrative on top.

Access

Key-authenticated API

Submit a job, poll for status, pull the result once it's ready. Built as an async queue from the start, not bolted on after.
